Methodology

Our Proven 4-Step Process

Hover over each step to see how we guarantee a flawless and durable finish on every single property.

1. Deep Surface Preparation

We don't just paint over problems. We scrape, sand, and chemically clean every inch to ensure absolute adhesion.

2. Multi-Layer Waterproofing

Applying elastomeric coatings and high-grade sealants to block out all moisture and prevent future structural damage.

3. Precision Paint Application

Using state-of-the-art sprayers and premium rollers to apply smooth, even coats of highly durable, weather-resistant paint.

4. Client Walkthrough

A rigorous final inspection with you to guarantee that every single corner meets the ProCoat golden standard.
