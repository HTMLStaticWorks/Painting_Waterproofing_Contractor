The ProCoat Standard

Anatomy of a Perfect Finish

Every layer matters. Here is a breakdown of the exact process and materials we use to ensure your property remains beautiful and protected for over a decade.

1. Deep Penetrating Primer

We apply specialized alkaline-resistant primers that bond chemically with the plaster, preventing efflorescence and peeling.

2. Multi-Polymer Waterproofing

Before painting, we apply highly flexible elastomeric crack-fillers to bridge structural gaps and seal out all moisture.

3. Weather-Defying Base Coat

A thick, protective mid-coat is applied to create a robust barrier against harsh UV rays, rain, and extreme temperature shifts.

4. High-Performance Top Coat

The final finish. Formulated with anti-dust and anti-algae properties to ensure vibrant, non-fading colors that self-clean.
