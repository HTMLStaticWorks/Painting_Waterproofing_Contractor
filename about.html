Our Journey

How We Grew

From a small local crew to the region's most trusted painting and waterproofing contractor.

2014

The Beginning

ProCoat was founded by a small team of passionate painters with a single truck and a commitment to absolute quality.

2017

Waterproofing Division

Recognizing a massive need in the market, we partnered with Dr. Fixit and launched our specialized exterior waterproofing division.

2021

Commercial Expansion

We scaled our operations to take on large commercial projects, providing weatherproofing for offices, malls, and warehouses.

2026

Industry Leaders

Today, ProCoat is recognized as the #1 painting and waterproofing contractor in the region, having successfully transformed over 5,000 properties.
